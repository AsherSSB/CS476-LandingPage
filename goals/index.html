Our Goals & Vision

Our Mission

Generous ecommerce connectors aims to empower retailers by developing robust plugins for Shopify and Salesforce, enabling seamless transmission of catalog data directly to Generous. This integration paves the way for the adoption of agentic commerce protocols, revolutionizing how retailers connect and transact in the ecommerce ecosystem.

Core Objectives

Technical Excellence

  • Develop robust plugins for Shopify and Salesforce
  • Enable direct catalog data transmission to Generous
  • Implement agentic commerce protocol support
  • Ensure plugin reliability and security

Retailer Integration

  • Simplify plugin installation and configuration
  • Provide real-time catalog data synchronization
  • Enable seamless agentic commerce protocol adoption
  • Offer comprehensive integration support

Business Growth

  • Establish partnerships with Shopify and Salesforce
  • Generate revenue through plugin licensing
  • Expand to additional ecommerce platforms
  • Build brand in plugin development and integration

Key Features & Capabilities

Shopify Plugin Development

Our dedicated Shopify plugin enables retailers to seamlessly transmit catalog data directly to Generous, streamlining inventory management and enhancing connectivity.

Salesforce Plugin Development

Our Salesforce integration plugin facilitates direct catalog data transmission, empowering retailers with robust CRM and ecommerce synchronization capabilities.

Catalog Data Integration

Enable retailers to send comprehensive product catalog data directly to Generous, ensuring accurate and up-to-date inventory information across platforms.

Agentic Commerce Protocol

Prepare for the future with support for agentic commerce protocols, enabling autonomous and intelligent ecommerce interactions between retailers and platforms.
